Review UI implementations for design consistency, responsiveness, accessibility, and edge-case resilience.

## Checklist

1. Visual hierarchy and token alignment (colors, font ramps, spacing).
2. States: Empty, Loading, Error, Hover, Focus, Disabled, Overflow.
3. Contrast ratios (WCAG AA) and typography legibility.
